- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 3D (the job's network), SDD 31
  (the network server) and the plane's data-path rules (DP-I4: nothing writes a buffer while
  another party holds it). **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`.
- **Found by:** 6.3ab's counters in run 10 (results 202611190756, `node/*.meters.json`). These

## The defect

Two code paths, found by 6.3ab's reading and confirmed by the counts:
1. **`Mapping::read` and `Mapping::write` copy one byte at a time**, with a volatile access each
   (`kernel/sys/src/lib.rs`, about line 693). Every byte a client receives is copied into the
   client's loan this way, and the session copies it out again the same way.
2. **Every receive still pending is tried again at every poll** (`kernel/net/stack/src/stack.rs`,
   `try_receive_into`), and its loan is mapped and unmapped on each try. That is 35 tries for each
   one answered.

## Decided (PM, 2026-11-19)

1. **Copies through a mapping are bulk copies.**
   - `Mapping::read` and `write` copy with `core::ptr::copy_nonoverlapping` over the mapped range,
     not byte-wise volatile accesses.
   - This is sound only while no other party writes the range during the copy. The loan contract
     gives that: a client does not touch a loan between handing it over and its answer, which is
     DP-I4's rule for the plane.
   - Write that invariant at the call sites and in SDD 31. Where a caller cannot promise it, keep
     a word-wise volatile copy (8 bytes an access) for that caller, and name it.
2. **A pending receive is tried when its socket has data**, not at every poll. Use smoltcp's
   readiness (`can_recv`) or the stack's own ready set. Its loan stays mapped from the receive's
   arrival to its answer, so each try neither maps nor unmaps.
3. **Measure again** with the same counters before anything else:
   - `heap_maps` is a follow-up only if it still costs a significant share of `cpu_us`;
   - the netvsc drops and backlog are the next item.

## Refused

- Larger loans or chunks as the fix.
- More network-server threads before the copies are bulk.
- A copy path only the engine uses.

## Done when

- **Host tests fail first:**
  - a 64 MiB receive through a loan costs at least 5 times less in `to_clients_us` (or wall, on a
    host) than byte-wise;
  - a pending receive on a socket with no data is not tried at a poll (count the tries).
- **Existing tests pass:** the network server, session and sys tests, and the TLS suites.
- **Coverage:** at least 80% of the lines added.
- **Clippy** is clean on host and `x86_64-moruna-user`.
- **The PM's node run:** `to_clients` MB/s and Occupancy against run 10.
