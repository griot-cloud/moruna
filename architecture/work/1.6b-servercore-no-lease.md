# 1.6b: Why the node powers off on Servercore, found without the cloud

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, sections 1B (boot, the 30 s lease rule), 1C (network), 1D (config) and 1E (Servercore).
- **Needs:** 1.6's runs (below). **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`. You touch no cloud; the PM runs anything on Servercore.

## What the PM's runs on Servercore ru-3 showed (2026-11-18)

- The qcow2 image (`~/moruna-data/images/78c3b314-servercore/`) is accepted with UEFI and q35. The firmware loads our loader.
- On a private network (subnet 192.168.77.0/24, Neutron DHCP, a router to `external-network`, a floating IP), the server goes ACTIVE, never answers on 443, and is SHUTOFF about 20 s after ACTIVE. That is about 30 s after the node starts, which is 1B's "no lease within 30 s" fatal.
- **The console is unreadable:**
  - `console log show` answers 404 "Guest does not have a console available" for our image, with or without `hw_disk_bus=virtio`, `hw_vif_model=virtio` and `os_type=linux`.
  - Stock Ubuntu does get a console log: booted BIOS, and booted UEFI/q35 from a volume.
  - On that UEFI Ubuntu, the disk sat on a virtio-SCSI controller (`Pci(0x2,0x2)/…/Scsi(0x0,0x0)`). Servercore's own images set `hw_disk_bus=scsi` and `hw_scsi_model=virtio-scsi`.
- Logs: `~/moruna-data/servercore/run-*.log`, `uefi-check.log` and `console-check.log`.

## Do

1. **Reproduce a Servercore-like machine under QEMU** in one lock turn, using `moruna-arm` and the VM lock: q35 with OVMF, and our image's disk as virtio-blk, then as virtio-scsi. The NIC is virtio-net-pci both modern-only (`disable-legacy=on`) and transitional. The network is a tap or bridge with dnsmasq serving DHCP like Neutron's: MTU option 1450, a router option, and a classless static route to 169.254.169.254 the way Neutron sends it. Find which of these stops the node getting a lease, or stops it booting.
2. **Read Nova's libvirt driver:** under what conditions does `get_console_output` raise ConsoleNotAvailable for a UEFI guest? Compare the domain an image like ours gets with what Ubuntu's gets. Say what would give our image a console log, if anything.
3. **Name the cause** in Moruna's code (or the image), with a test that shows it under QEMU, and propose the fix. Stop and report before fixing. A missing driver (virtio-scsi) is a design decision for the PM.
