# 6.3x: a ring task stalls at the stage's drain, then releases a batch with no record

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 3A (the ring stage) and SDD 29
  (the plane). **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`, and 6.3's steps: write the defect as a
  test, then **stop and report** with a proposal before fixing.
- **Found by:** run 6 (results 202611190418, image cc3086ad, node alone, 4 CPUs, gate tasks
  built at 1fceede8). 6.3r's gap fix (dc352acc) did not end it.

## The evidence

Occupancy failed 2 of 6 runs (r2, r4) and Audit 2 of 6 (r1, r2). Every failure is the same:

1. **The source finishes** at about 17 s, and the function stage goes to `draining`.
2. **The workers stall.** The stage sits `Idle` with all 4 workers active for about 44 s: the
   bottleneck timeline ends `[44.39, 'Idle']`. There is no wait note; `WAIT_NOTE_NS` is 60 s,
   and `offer_timed`'s step/wait_for_room loop prints none.
3. **Then it fails**, at about 60 to 62 s after the stage started:
   "kernel stage 1 morsel N: image `gate-…`: it said a batch was done without a record for it"
   (`answers_timed`, `crates/moruna-micro/src/task.rs`).
4. **One task in each failure** has one more batch than records. The others report nothing.

| Run | Task: batches, records |
|---|---|
| occupancy r2 | task 2: 3, 2; task 3: 24, 23 |
| occupancy r4 | task 0: 256, 256 (it ended: "answered 256 batches"); task 1: 3, 2 |
| audit r1 | task 2: 150, 149 |
| audit r2 | task 2: 34, 33 |

5. **The output is short.** Rows out at the stall were 57,559,681 against 58,497,609 in a good
   run.

Good runs drain in about 1 to 2 s. Enrich (368 batches a task in r1) and Score never fail, so a
counter wrapping at 256 is not the cause.

**Ruled out by reading:**
- The plane orders a task's records before its release: `ringed.rs` `pump`, "the releases are
  read before the records, and given after them".
- `keep_in_place` bounds the windows the engine holds.

**Leads, not yet tested:**
- `answers_timed` drops a release for a batch other than the one taken (`Seen::Released(_) => {}`).
- `step_timed` drops one when nothing is outstanding.
- A task with a batch in flight at the drain may have no unit come for it until `finish`, while
  its records hold its windows.
- Something on the task side may end its wait at about 60 s. Read the task library the gate tasks
  were built with (1fceede8, `kernel/sys/src/plane.rs`: `wait_window`, `step_parts`) beside the
  engine's.

## Do

1. **Reproduce it.** Use the host model of the ring stage (3.1's, `sc_p_native_stage_memory`) or a
   booted 4-CPU Occupancy or Audit month in `moruna-arm` (lock turn), looping until it fails.
2. **Make the failure say what it saw.** When a release finds no record, the error names:
   - the batch;
   - `in_flight`, `taking` and `early`;
   - the task's windows (`window_states`);
   - the last events.

   Also give `offer_timed`'s wait a note at the stage's wait-note interval, as `wait_any_timed`
   has.
3. **Write the defect as a failing test**, name the cause in the code, then **stop and report**
   with the fix proposed and its alternatives.

## Refused

- A retry of the unit.
- A longer timeout.
- Treating a release with no record as an empty answer.

## Report

The reproduction, the cause, the test, and the proposed fix with alternatives.
