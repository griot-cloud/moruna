# 6.3c: A read in flight is counted twice by the arena's admission

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 3A (the arena bounds how far reading
  runs ahead).
- **Needs:** 6.3a (merged, caf2112d). **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`, and 6.3's steps.

## The miss

Found by 6.3a's measurement on the Mac: the year file into a count, 2 CPUs, a budget of 672 MiB.

- Workers still idle 0.5–0.9 s of about 3 s.
- One read was in flight for 2.29 s of 4.68 s. 1,832 of 1,858 refusals came with one read in flight
  and a median room of 0.
- **The cause:** a whole-group read in flight is counted twice. Its pending charge is 312 MB, and the
  arena's in-use also includes the bytes it has already taken: its fetch, plus the arena buffers its
  decode reserved, about 172 MB. A second read finds no room until the first lands.
- It is not the start's ramp and not mainly the read target.

## Do

1. **Write the defect as a test:** a read's charge and the arena bytes it has taken are never both
   counted. While a read is in flight, admission sees `max(charge, taken)`, never `charge + taken`.
   It fails today.
2. **Fix it in the accounting,** not in a constant. Either a read's pending charge shrinks by what it
   has allocated (per-read tracking), or its decode's buffers are reserved from the read's own claim.
   Choose the one that keeps G-I2's single copy and 6.3a's batch decode, say why, and report before
   committing if it changes any public type.
3. **Rerun 6.3a's host table** at 672 MiB, 1 GiB and 1280 MiB, with wall, idle and peak, and the
   unbudgeted parquet → parquet no-regression runs.

## Refused

A smaller rounding factor; a constant tuned to 672 MiB.

## Report

The test, the fix and why that one, the table, coverage and the commits.
