# 6.3af: netvsc queues and drops frames because the network server takes them one answer at a time

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 1F (VMBus, netvsc) and 3D, SDD 31
  (the network server) and the card protocol (`kernel/drivers/net-protocol`, the hub).
  **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`.
- **Found by:** run 11's meters (results 202611190853, `node/*.meters.json`), after 6.3ad brought
  the network server's copies to its clients to about 1 GB/s.

## The evidence

netvsc's totals at the node's 467th second, nearly all of it the benchmark:

| Counter | Value | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| `in` | 8.17M frames, 10.04 GB | |
| `answered` / `answered_frames` | 238,105 answers, 8.13M frames | about 34 frames an answer |
| `backlog` | 154,399 of 238,105 answers | 65% of answers left frames queued behind them |
| `dropped` | 34,773 frames (0.43%) | each one a TCP retransmit and a smaller window |
| `sections_full` | 3,102 | sends waited for a free send section |

Over one 65 s window around Occupancy r5: 79% of answers were backlogged and 16,369 frames were
dropped. A read still averages about 7 MB/s a request, against the network server's 1 GB/s to its
clients.

From 6.3ab's reading of the code:
- the network server keeps **one** receive outstanding on `net0`;
- the hub answers it with at most 65,472 B, 43 full frames;
- a new receive goes out only after that answer is processed.

So frames arriving while an answer is in flight wait in the hub's queue, and are dropped when it is
full.

## Decided (PM, 2026-11-19)

1. **The network server keeps receives outstanding on the card so that one is always armed while
   it processes another.** Each answer goes on carrying what the hub has queued, up to its limit.
   - The count is set by the card's receive ring, never by a constant. It is the least that keeps
     `backlog` and `dropped` at zero at the hub's queue limit: the queue's frames over an answer's.
     Say in SDD 31 how it is derived.
2. **Name where frames are dropped** (the hub's queue, netvsc's receive ring, or the host's) before
   anything else changes. If the drops are not the hub's queue, **stop and report**.
3. **`sections_full` on sends** is noted, not fixed here.

## Refused

- A larger answer as the only fix: the round trip still serialises.
- A bigger queue that hides the wait.
- Raising the MTU.

## Done when

- **A host test fails first:** the hub with a burst of frames larger than one answer, while one
  receive is outstanding. Today it backlogs (and drops past the queue limit); after the fix, none.
- **Existing tests pass:** netvsc, net-protocol, netstack and the TLS suites.
- **Coverage:** at least 80% of the lines added.
- **Clippy** is clean on host and `x86_64-moruna-user`.
- **The PM's node run:** `backlog`, `dropped` and the reads' MB/s a request against run 11.
