# 3.1: The full-year hang

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 3 (item 3.1).
- **Needs:** nothing to start; a booted proof waits for the VM lock. **Rules:**
  `architecture/work/_rules.md`.

## Goal

Find why a job stops making progress, and bring the PM a fix to approve before you build it.

## What is known

- On tag `gate-verified-2026-11-17` (6c9dd70a), the full-year NYC Score run (41.2M rows, 8 native
  tasks, budget 19 GiB) produced no output and was killed at 900 s, at 2.5% CPU.
- One month of Score at a 2 GiB budget stalled twice before that: first with 30% of its rows
  inside the stage, then, after `c512249c` ("a unit leaves no piece queued, so a stage under memory
  pressure drains"), before its source finished (181,688 rows, then nothing).
- The integrator's reading: a deadlock between the per-task queue (stream P) and the controller's
  memory bound. Batches sit queued while the bound blocks the work that would free them.
- Unmerged work in progress: `a685ae9c` on `feature/data-os-p-4b0c77` (a report written on cancel,
  and the budget-2 stall). Read it; don't assume it is right.
- The record: `architecture/data-os-streams.md`'s integration log, and
  `architecture/arrow-core-0.7.md` section 9.

## Build

1. **Reproduce on the host first.** A test in `moruna-scheduler` or `moruna-micro` that drives a
   native stage with 8 tasks under a memory bound, and stops making progress the way the runs did.
   No VM.
2. **Find the cause,** with the line or lines at fault, and why the earlier fix didn't cover it.
3. **Stop and report:** the test, the cause, and the fix you propose, with what it changes. Wait for
   the PM's approval.
4. **After approval:** the fix, the test passing, and every scheduler, micro and runtime test still
   passing.

## Success

- A host test that fails before the fix and passes after it.
- After the fix, one month of Score at 2 GiB and at 19 GiB completes under the emulator, booted
  with CI's machine. That boot needs the VM lock: queue for it, never take it from a holder.
- The full-year proof on all four jobs needs a KVM machine, so it runs later, as item 3.8's
  precondition.

## Not yours

Speed. Any change to the plane's ABI without asking. The node's image profile.

## Report

After step 3: the test, the cause and the proposed fix. After step 4: the commits and results.
