# 4.6: `mlp_forward`

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, sections 4A (a declared tolerance), 4B,
  4C (configure, then process), 4G.1. **Needs:** 4.1's formats. **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`.

## Build

1. **The specification:**
   - the model handle: layers, weights and biases, and activations (at least ReLU, sigmoid, tanh
     and identity), fixed at configure;
   - inputs are k Float columns or one `FixedSizeList<Float>`; outputs are Float or
     `FixedSizeList<Float>`;
   - the declared precision (float32 or float64) and the tolerance a device implementation may
     differ by, stated against float64;
   - null inputs are stated.
2. **The CPU reference:** a blocked matrix multiply over row blocks, with the weights held across
   batches, using an existing well-tested Rust linear-algebra crate if one fits the workspace's
   `cargo deny` rules (say which, and why), otherwise its own blocked loop.
3. **The corpus,** including Score's network from `bench/vm/workloads.py` (23-128-128-1), with its
   features, weights and expected outputs.

## Measure (4G.1)

Score's network at 10^4, 10^5 and 10^6 rows, float32 and float64, on the Mac's CPU and on one
core of moruna-arm (in a VM lock turn), against NumPy's float64 as the benchmark computes it. Record
the error against float64.

## Success

- Score through `expr` for its features (or plain Arrow until 4.2 lands, said so) and
  `mlp_forward` gives 0.6.6's rows within the declared tolerance, and bit for bit in float64.
- The measurement, and coverage of 80% or more.

## Report

The spec, the reference and the crate chosen, the corpus, the measurement, coverage and the
commits.
