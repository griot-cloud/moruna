# 6.3b: A morsel larger than its tier's budget stalls the job

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, sections 3A (the job's memory) and 6C.
- **Needs:** 6.3a (same crates; the same agent, after it). **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`,
  and 6.3's steps (`architecture/work/6.3-each-miss.md`).

## The miss

Found by 3.8's proof under QEMU (2026-11-18), on a cloud node of 4 GiB with 4 CPUs:

- **The job:** Enrich (`bench/vm/workloads.py`) on 2024-01, from a volume to a volume. The budget was
  the node's `job_memory_bytes`, 2.54 GiB. The engine started with 1852 MiB of arena and 617 MiB of
  scratch.
- **The result:** exit 1 after 29 s, with `staging: morsel 0 (235894392 bytes) ... more than a
  budget of 223379456 holds ... the run has no disk tier`.
- Score, Occupancy and Audit passed on the same node, with 0.6.6's rows and hashes.
- **Where it fails:** `crates/moruna-scheduler/src/decide.rs` (the push loop). A morsel is refused
  by its tier's budget, it waits for room, the spill fails (a node has no disk tier), and after
  `PUSH_STALL` the job fails. Here the morsel is larger than the tier's whole budget, so no room
  could ever come.

## Do

6.3's steps, on the Mac with the engine on the host:

1. **Reproduce it** with the same job and budget (Enrich on the January month at a 2.54 GiB budget,
   or the smallest budget that shows it), and say which tier's budget is 223,379,456 and how it is
   derived.
2. **Name the cause:** why a morsel is made larger than a tier it must pass through, for example a
   read target set from the arena alone, or a function (Enrich adds columns) growing a morsel past
   what was planned.
3. **Write the defect in general terms,** with a test that shows it without this job. The rule to
   test: no morsel is ever larger than the smallest budget of the tiers it must be placed in, and a
   morsel that would be is cut by rows, not stalled.
4. **Stop and report to the PM** before fixing.

## Refused

Raising the tier's budget for this job; a constant tuned to Enrich or to 4 GiB; a disk tier on the
node, which the design does not give a job; anything special to Enrich.

## Report

The reproduction, the tier and its budget, the cause with file and function, the general defect, the
test, and the fix you propose.
