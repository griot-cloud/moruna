# 6.3d: The Parquet sink's writer heap is bounded by the budget

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 3A (the arena bounds what is in flight; bytes outside it stay within the arena sizing's room). **Needs:** 3.5b, 6.3a–c (merged). **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`, and 6.3's steps: stop and report before fixing.

## The miss

3.5b's measurement (the NYC month copied at a 342 MB budget) shows the Parquet sink's ArrowWriter is now the largest heap use outside the arena, at 40–55 MB. Each lane keeps its in-progress row group, and each column's pages and dictionary, on the heap. Its size comes from parquet's writer defaults × columns × lanes, not from the budget (`crates/moruna-sinks/src/parquet_sink.rs`, `encode_checked`).

2. Name the cause and write a failing test at a small budget: the writer heap must stay within what the arena sizing allows above the arena.
3. Stop and report, with the fix you propose: for example, row-group and page sizes derived from the budget and the lanes, or encoding into arena buffers.
