# 2.4: Job records carry times to the millisecond

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 2 (the API's job records and events). **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`.

## Why

6.2's harness reads a job's `submitted`, `started` and `ended` from the node, and they are whole seconds. On 20–95 s jobs that is ±1 s, and start-up (submitted → started) is mostly under a second, so the table cannot show it.

## Build

1. Job records and events carry `submitted_ms`, `started_ms` and `ended_ms`: Unix milliseconds from the node's one wall clock (3D). They sit beside the existing seconds fields, which stay for compatibility.
2. Update the API's schema, the generated OpenAPI and MCP descriptions, and the Python client, as their checks require. Never hand-edit generated code.
3. Host tests: each job's three times are millisecond-precise and ordered, and the seconds fields equal them divided by 1000.
4. `bench/node/` (6.2's harness) reads the ms fields when present.

## Report

The commits and the tests.
