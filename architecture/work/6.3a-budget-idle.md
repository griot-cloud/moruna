# 6.3a: A worker idles under the node's memory budget

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, sections 3A (the job's memory) and 6C.
- **Needs:** nothing new; 3.3 merged. **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`, and 6.3's steps
  (`architecture/work/6.3-each-miss.md`), which this item follows.

## The miss

Found by 3.3's boot (PM, 2026-11-18), before 6.2, so it is a 6.3 item of its own:

- **The job:** read one Parquet file of 1,125,878,149 bytes and 67,557,899 rows (NYC taxi 2024)
  into a `count` sink, on 2 CPUs under QEMU TCG.
- **The budgets:**
  - With the job's whole 1 GiB as arena, it took 48.65 s.
  - With 3A's split of the same 1 GiB (672 MiB arena plus 224 MiB scratch, as node-admission
    gives it), it took 83.34 s.
  - The driver path and the direct path behave the same.
- **What it shows:** at 672 MiB, one of the two workers sits idle for about 40 to 60 s. A third
  less arena nearly halves the throughput of a job that only counts rows. That is a defect until
  shown otherwise. A count holds almost nothing, so the arena should bound how far reading runs
  ahead, not how many workers decode.

## Do

6.3's steps, on the Mac with the engine on the host. It is not a TCG artifact until a host run
says so.

1. **Reproduce it.** Use the same kind of file: one large Parquet file of the NYC 2024 data, at
   least 1 GiB, made from `~/moruna-data/nyc` or the normalised files the gate uses. Use the same
   budgets: arena and scratch as 3A splits 1 GiB, against the whole 1 GiB as arena, with 2
   workers. Report the wall time, and each worker's busy and idle time from the run's report or
   trace.
2. **Name the cause in Moruna's code:** the file, the function, and why a worker waits. For
   example, an admission that reserves a row group's whole decoded size, a read-ahead that holds
   the arena, or a refusal that parks a worker instead of letting it work on something smaller.
3. **Write the defect in general terms,** with a test that shows it without this file: a host
   test or a small job where two workers under a budget do the work of one.
4. **Stop and report to the PM** before fixing. Report the measurements, the cause, the defect
   and the test.

## Refused

Constants tuned to this file or to 1 GiB; changing 3A's split to hide it; anything special to
`count`. If the only fix is one of these, say so and stop.

## Report

The reproduction table, the cause with file and function, the general defect, the test, and the
fix you propose.
