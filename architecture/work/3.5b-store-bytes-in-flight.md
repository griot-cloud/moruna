# 3.5b: The object stores' bytes in flight are counted against the job's memory

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, sections 3A and 3D.
- **Needs:** 3.5 and 6.3a. **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`, and 6.3's steps
  (`architecture/work/6.3-each-miss.md`): stop and report before fixing.

## The miss

Found by 3.5's lock turn (2026-11-18). The test copied the NYC 2024-01 month store to store, with a
job memory limit of 342,016,000 bytes and an arena of 201 MB:

- **Blob:** peaked at 0.96 of the limit.
- **S3:** peaked at 0.88 in its passing run. Another run was killed at the limit with "account
  runtime has no room for 4128 bytes", leaving 9 part files and no report.
- **The growth is outside the arena:** range bodies being received, upload parts being sent, and the
  TLS and connector buffers.

**The rule (3A, from 6.3a):** a read's bytes held outside the arena never exceed its own bytes in
flight, so that the arena sizing's room above the arena holds them. An object store's ranges and
parts in flight are reads and writes like any other. Their number and size are derived from the
budget, never from fixed defaults (`parallel_ranges`, `range_bytes`, `parallel_parts`,
`part_bytes`).

## Do

1. **Measure** where the bytes above the arena go in one Blob copy and one S3 copy: range bodies,
   parts, TLS and connector buffers, and the source's or sink's own. Use the host (the engine against
   Azurite and MinIO on the Mac, or `moruna-arm` in a lock turn) at the same budget.
2. **Name the cause:** which of them is unbounded by the budget, and where in the code.
3. **Write the defect in general terms,** with a failing test at a small budget.
4. **Stop and report to the PM** with the fix you propose. One candidate: a range GET is charged to
   the arena before it is issued and its body lands in that charge, and uploads are bounded the
   same way.

## Refused

Raising the limit for the copy; a constant tuned to this month or this budget.

## Report

The measurements, the cause, the defect, the test, and the fix you propose.
