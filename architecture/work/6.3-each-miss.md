# 6.3: Each miss, through 6C

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 6C.
- **Needs:** one miss from 6.2. **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`.

One agent per miss. The PM gives the job, the engine it lost to, and the losing stage.

## Do

1. **Profile the losing stage** on a node under QEMU or on the Mac (the engine on the host), with
   the same data. Name the cause in Moruna's code: the file, the function, and why it is slow.
2. **Write the defect in general terms:** what is broken or inefficient, which workloads besides
   this job it hurts, and a test that shows it without the benchmark (a host test or a small job).
3. **Stop and report to the PM** before fixing. The PM approves the fix, or asks for more.
4. **Fix it,** with the test, at the 80% floor.

## Refused

Constants tuned to a dataset; a path special to a job or a benchmark; anything that changes what
is measured or how. If the only fix you can find is one of these, say so and stop.

## Report

The cause, the general defect, the test, and after approval the fix's commits. The PM reruns 6.2's
job and logs it in `architecture/benchmark-log.md`.
