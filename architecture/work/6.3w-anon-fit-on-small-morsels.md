# 6.3w: the controller's anon fit charges small morsels for the process's own growth

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 3A (a function's memory is
  measured, not assumed) and SDD 11 f.3 (the two-term out-of-arena fit). **Rules:**
  `architecture/work/_rules.md`.
- **Found by:** 6.3f (IM-T32 and IM-T33, 1 GiB, `arrow_echo`).
  - On 5 KB batches the anon fit read 115 to 216 bytes per morsel byte, with a fixed term of 0.
    The controller then ran 1 or 2 workers of 4, with 66 MB of anon for kernels.
  - On 1 MiB batches it read 12.9, where the truth is about 2: the process's own ~3.9 MB was put
    on the morsels. Workers dropped to 1 at the warm-up's end and came back one every 0.27 s.
  - Node boots show "anon fit 0 bytes fixed plus ~4 bytes a morsel byte" as well.

## The defect

`fit_anon` (`crates/moruna-controller/src/lib.rs`) fits the slope to each morsel's growth alone.
When the window cannot resolve a line (fewer than `MIN_ANON_POINTS` readings, or less than
`ANON_SPREAD` of spread in `x`), the fallback takes the steepest `growth / x` in the window. A
run's records all arrive at one target, so it never has the spread.

The growth across one `apply` also holds what does not scale with the morsel: an allocator's
chunk, first touches, a thread's buffers. On a small morsel that fixed growth divided by `x`
becomes a slope 50 to 100 times the true one, and `anon_footprint` charges it once per morsel in
flight. So the only remedy left is fewer workers.

The run already has the spread it lacks. The warm-up measures the function's line over pieces of
1 to 16 MiB: on the node (6.3g, `Measured::from_readings`, its fixed term and per-byte slope) and
on Linux (6.3f, the child's `memory.peak`). The job or the store may also carry a measured line.

## Decided (PM, 2026-11-19)

1. **The controller is given the stage's measured line** where one exists: its fixed bytes and
   its bytes per input byte, in the same units the runtime sizes with (`fund_measured`).
   - Carry it the way the controller already receives what it is told about a kernel before it
     probes (`KernelHints`, or the stage's probe result), not through a new channel.
   - Where no line exists, nothing changes.
2. **With a measured line, the window's no-spread fallback takes the line's slope**, not the
   steepest `growth / x`. The constant keeps its rule: the larger, at every reading, of the
   residency before `apply` less the other morsels' share, and what the reading needs above the
   line. So every reading in the window is still bounded (RC-I1), and the growth that does not
   scale goes into `c`, once.
3. **A window with the spread fits its own line, as today.** The measured line seeds it and does
   not override it.
4. **The summary line says which slope stands:** "anon fit … (slope from the stage's measured
   line)" or "(fitted)" or "(steepest reading)".

## Refused

- A floor or cap on the slope in bytes per byte.
- A minimum morsel size for the fit.
- Dropping the readings of small morsels.
- A constant subtracted from the growth.

## Done when

- **The defect fails first.** IM-T32 and IM-T33's figures become a host test of the controller:
  5 KB and 1 MiB morsels with a measured line of about 2 bytes a byte and about 4 MB fixed. Under
  today's rule it fails: the slope reads 100 or more and the workers drop to 1. With the fix the
  slope is the line's, the workers stay at what the arena and CPU allow, and every reading is
  still bounded.
- **Existing tests pass**, the S1 and RC-I1 tests included.
- **Coverage** of the lines added is at least 80%.
- **The Linux check (the helper):** IM-T32 and IM-T33 rerun in `moruna-arm`, reporting the workers
  and the fit's summary line.
- **The node check (the PM).**

### Decided at the helper's stop (PM, 2026-11-19)

The host part is built: 3d60bfb6 holds RC-T27, and 57f7f5fb carries the job, registry or store
line through `KernelHints::measured_line`. The warm-up's line is fitted after the probe, so it
reaches the controller by neither of those.

- **The warm-up's line arrives mid-run, on the stage's per-tick counters.** A `Kernel` method is
  read into `StageStats`, the way `morsel_bytes_max` brings the warm-up's cap (RC-I11). The
  controller sets the line and refits at once.
- **The probe's slope is seeded from the line when one exists.** A single probe reading has no
  spread.
- **Refused:**
  - the line helping only a later run;
  - carrying it on `ProbeResult`, which misses small batches;
  - probing across the warm-up, which changes the probe's contract.
