# 6.3v: on Linux, an image instance its cgroup kills is started again, as the node's task is

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, section 3A (a function's memory is
  measured; a task that passes its room starts again at a larger account) and 3E (one instance per
  CPU). **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`.
- **Needs:** 6.3f (merged, b7ffda21: one instance per CPU, a cgroup each, the warm-up on the child's
  `memory.peak`) and 6.3g (merged, 13bb3750: the node's restart, `TaskState::restart` in
  `crates/moruna-micro/src/task.rs`, `layout::restarted_limit`).
- **Why:** Moruna behaves the same on a host, a VM and the microkernel (memory
  production-is-one-fixed-node). On the node, a task that passes its account starts again at a
  larger one and the job completes. On Linux, an image instance that its cgroup kills
  (`memory.events` `oom_kill`) fails the job (`crates/moruna-sandbox/src/image/mod.rs`,
  `failure_head` with `oom`).

## The defect

`ImageKernel` (`crates/moruna-sandbox/src/image/`) reports an instance killed by its cgroup as the
unit's error (`oom_kills() > 0` gives "killed by its memory limit"). The unit, and the job, fail.
The node in the same case retires the task, carves a new one at the grown account, and posts its
unanswered pieces again.

## Do

1. **Write the defect as a test** (Linux, delegated cgroup v2, as 6.3f's tests are). An image
   function stateless (`ResumePolicy::Reinit`) whose first batch's working set is larger than its
   measured account is killed by its cgroup. The job must complete with every row, and the
   stage's note must say the instance started again.
2. **One rule, in one place.** Move `restarted_limit` and `measured_task_limit` from
   `crates/moruna-micro/src/layout.rs` into `moruna_adapters::instances`, beside
   `measured_pieces`, where 6.3f put the shared floor. `moruna-micro` calls them there, with no
   behaviour change: its tests pass unchanged. The rule is the node's:
   - grow the account to the larger of twice it and the need widened by the measurement's margin;
   - the cap is under half the room;
   - it must grow, or the instance is not started again.
3. **The Linux restart (`ImageKernel`).** When an instance's process ends and its cgroup's
   `oom_kill` count rose, and the kernel's resume policy is `Reinit`:
   - **The need** is the cgroup's `memory.peak` at the kill (read before the cgroup goes).
   - **The room** is what the run's ceiling leaves the stage with this instance's limit returned.
   - **The new account** is `instances::restarted_limit(limit, peak, margin, room)`, with the
     margin the warm-up measured.
   - **On `None`**, or for a kernel that keeps state, the unit fails with today's error.
   - **Otherwise:** start a fresh process and cgroup at the grown limit; send the batch it was
     answering, and any it had not answered, again in order; keep the answers already taken.

   Both paths feed the same note. Its format is the node's: "task N passed its account of …
   started again with an account of … bytes, its K unanswered pieces posted again". The node's
   report shows it as the node's does. A second kill of the same instance follows the same rule
   from the grown account. There is no separate count of restarts and no constant.
4. **The other instances** keep their accounts. The grown one is the instance's from then on, as
   on the node (`restarted`).

## Refused

- A retry count.
- A fixed headroom in bytes.
- Raising every instance's limit when one is killed.
- Restarting a stateful image.

## Done when

- The test passes on Linux with every row equal to a run with room to spare.
- 6.3f's and 6.3g's tests pass.
- Coverage of the added lines is at least 80%.
- x86_64 clippy is clean.
- The report says which instance restarted and at what account.

The PM runs the node and Linux check.
