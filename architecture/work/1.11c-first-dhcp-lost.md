# 1.11c: The first DHCP exchange is lost on Azure

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, sections 1B (one interface, DHCP; RFC 2131
  timing) and 1F (Azure, netvsc).
- **Needs:** 1.11b (merged). **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`.

## The miss

On real Azure (`Standard_D2s_v4`, griot-dev), the node's first DHCP exchange is lost in 3 of the
last 5 boots:

| Run | Lease | boot_ms |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | about 10 s (smoltcp's old 10 s retry) | 11,697 |
| 9 | about 10 s (smoltcp's old 10 s retry) | 10,751 |
| 10 | 47 ms | 1,772 |
| 11 | 4,034 ms after the network server started (the new 4 s retry) | 4,719 |

5bd6b7ff's RFC 2131 timing bounds the cost at 4 s, but Linux on Azure gets its lease on the first
try. Something about Moruna's first DISCOVER, or the reply to it, is lost. The consoles are in
`~/moruna-data/azure/console-2026-11-18T*`. In each, netvsc reports `link up` and `served as net0`
about 200 ms after its start.

## Do

1. **Instrument, in the image only while diagnosing** (an `image` feature or a debug netvsc build,
   never shipped). For the first 20 frames each way, log with times on netvsc's own clock:
   - each frame netvsc is asked to send: its length, and whether it is DHCP;
   - when each send completes;
   - each frame received;
   - when the packet filter, link up and `served as net0` happen.

   This should show whether the DISCOVER left the guest and whether an OFFER came back.
2. **The PM runs it on Azure** (`azure-up.sh`) until a boot loses the first exchange. Give the PM
   the image command.
3. **Name the cause** in the code, then fix it there, with a host test where the netvsc library can
   show it. Candidates to check:
   - a send before the host has completed the packet filter or the link;
   - the first send's completion never processed;
   - an OFFER received before the network server listens;
   - a broadcast or destination MAC, or a checksum, the host's switch drops on the first frame.

## Scope, widened (PM, 2026-11-18)

After the DISCOVER fix (1b7391b9), 10 restarts gave boot_ms 2.0 to 8.7 s, clustered at whole
seconds that DHCP's randomized retries cannot make, which smoltcp's 1 s ARP and SYN timers and
`platform`'s 1 s instance-data retry can. The item now covers the boot's whole network path:
the goal is the shortest time from the card's start to `ready` on Azure, not just the lease.
The trace image follows DHCP, ARP and TCP's SYN and RST each way, every RNDIS indication, and
the media connect status asked before and after the packet filter, and states 802.1Q and
teaming in the NDIS configuration, as Linux does; a REQUEST that follows an OFFER now takes
the DISCOVER's schedule. `platform`'s retries and smoltcp's ARP and SYN timers are in scope
if the trace shows those losses. The PM restates the criterion once the trace's data is in.

## Success

10 Azure boots in a row whose lease comes within 2 s of the network server's start, as the PM
runs them. (Was 1 s. The trace of 2026-11-18, `console-trace-1.log`, showed Azure dropping the
first DISCOVER with no signal when the path opens, so a boot that loses it leases at the first
retransmission, about 1 s; the PM's decision.)

## Report

The instrumented console of a boot that lost the first exchange, the cause, the fix and its test,
and the 10 boots.

## Closed (PM, 2026-11-18)

Trace run 4 had 11 Azure boots, D2s_v4, one create and 10 restarts. The consoles are in
`~/moruna-data/boot10/moruna-trace4/consoles/`; the analysis is in the PM's log.

- **Leases:** 6 boots leased 57–87 ms after the network server started, 1 at 1.1 s, and 4 at 2.8–3.1 s.
- **Why the slow boots were slow:** Azure dropped every DISCOVER until its path opened, which was
  up to about 3 s after the card started. Every dropped DISCOVER completed with success.
- **No sign the path is open:** there were no RNDIS indications, no media status change, and no
  inbound frame before the answered DISCOVER's OFFER.
- **No faster schedule pays:** doubling from 100–500 ms saves under 0.15 s on average. Only fixed
  250 ms retries save about 0.4 s, and they leave RFC 2131's backoff.

**Decision:**
- The schedule stays as 1b7391b9 and 5635604a set it.
- The 2 s criterion is withdrawn: the window is the host's, and the node gets its lease within one
  retransmission of the window's end.
- Item 1.11f takes the time the node itself loses after the lease.
