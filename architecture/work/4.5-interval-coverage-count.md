# 4.5: `interval_coverage_count`

- **System design:** `architecture/moruna-os-system.md`, sections 4A, 4B, 4G.2. **Needs:** 4.1's
  formats. **Rules:** `architecture/work/_rules.md`.

## Build

1. **The specification:**
   - inputs are group Int32, start Int64 and length Int64; the grid's step and size are parameters;
   - for each group and bucket, the number of intervals that cover it;
   - the output is (group, bucket, count UInt64) for non-zero cells, in key order;
   - the rules are stated for an interval that wraps the grid (Occupancy's minute of day wraps at
     midnight), for a zero or negative length, for nulls, and for a group outside a declared
     domain.
2. **The CPU reference:** add one at each interval's start bucket, subtract one past its end, then
   take a running sum per group. Partial results from several instances merge by addition.
3. **The corpus,** including hand-checked cases across the midnight wrap.

## Measure (4G.2)

Occupancy through this kernel, against Occupancy as written (expand each trip to one row per
minute, then count), on real months: wall time and peak memory. Its outputs must be equal, in the
same order.

## Success

- Occupancy through `interval_coverage_count` gives 0.6.6's rows bit for bit on a month and on the
  year.
- The measurement, and coverage of 80% or more.

## Report

The spec, the reference, the corpus, the measurement, coverage and the commits.
